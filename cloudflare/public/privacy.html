Privacy

Draft for review. This is a plain-language summary written by the product's builder, not legal advice. The operator of this service should review it before launch.

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What we collect

Your account: your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself). Sessions are stored as hashed tokens.

Your sites: the hostnames you add, and a hash of each site key.

Bot visits you send us: time, page path (without query string), the detected bot, the visitor's IP address, user agent, referrer (without query string), HTTP status, and country and network when the edge sensor provides them. Requests from people (non-bot user agents) are discarded.

What we do not collect

Cookies, authorization headers, query strings, or page content from your site.

How it is used

Only to show you your own dashboard. Your data is never shown to other accounts.

Retention and deletion

Visits are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can delete a site, or your whole account, from settings. Deleting removes everything recorded for it.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare's network (Workers and D1). Cloudflare acts as the hosting provider.

Contact

Contact the operator of this service at the address they publish for it.
