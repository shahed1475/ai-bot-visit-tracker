Questions

Will it slow down my site?

No. The edge sensor reports a visit after your page has been served, and visitors never wait for it. If the tracker is down, your site still loads.

Does it work if my site is not on Cloudflare?

Yes. Use the Node, Flask, Django or PHP middleware, or call the HTTPS endpoint from your own code. The edge sensor is only for sites that Cloudflare proxies.

What do you store?

For each bot visit: the time, page path, bot name, the visitor's IP address, user agent, status code and country. People are ignored. Query strings, cookies and authorization headers are never stored.

How does the IP check work?

OpenAI, Perplexity, Google, Bing, Apple and DuckDuckGo publish the IP ranges their crawlers use. A visit that claims to be one of them from a different IP is marked unverified. Anthropic publishes no ranges, so Claude visits show as unknown.

Can I block a bot from the dashboard?

Not yet. The dashboard shows who visits and why. To block a bot, add it to your robots.txt or your firewall.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a site, or your whole account, in settings. Everything recorded for it is removed.

Is there a limit?

Each account can add up to 5 sites.
